- "I pulled a video from the car last night"
- California = two-party consent state
- Recording without all parties' consent = felony
- **Remedy:** Police report + reference in all complaints

- Driver Luis eavesdropping + reporting to merchant
- Unauthorized access to client's private strategy information
- Supports fraud/conspiracy civil claims

- Pickup location downgrade (inside venue → CVS lot)
- Airport wait imposition without prior agreement
- Day Zero ride charged despite no confirmed pickup

### 4. Breach of Implied Covenant of Good Faith & Fair Dealing
- Unilateral contract term changes mid-performance
- Retroactive forfeiture rules never disclosed
- Gaslighting about authorization scope
- Threats + intimidation to coerce compliance

### 5. Unjust Enrichment

### 8. Intentional Infliction of Emotional Distress
- 4AM abandonment threat at unfamiliar venue
