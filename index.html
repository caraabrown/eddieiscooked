7. Legal Angles ▼

Legal Angles

1. CA Penal Code §632 (Illegal Recording)

"I pulled a video from the car last night" — California = two-party consent state. Recording without all parties' consent = felony. Remedy: Police report + reference in all complaints.

2. CA Penal Code §502 (Computer Fraud/Conspiracy)

Driver Luis eavesdropping + reporting to merchant = unauthorized access to client's private strategy information. Supports fraud/conspiracy civil claims.

3. Breach of Specific Performance Terms

Pickup location downgrade (inside venue → CVS lot), airport wait imposition without agreement, Day Zero ride charged despite no confirmed pickup.

4. Breach of Implied Covenant of Good Faith & Fair Dealing

Unilateral contract term changes mid-performance, retroactive forfeiture rules, gaslighting about authorization scope, threats + intimidation to coerce compliance.

5. Unjust Enrichment

Paid $1,100 for 12 rides; kept $1,100 for 7 degraded rides. Unjust enrichment: ~$400-450.

6. Conversion (Unlawful Taking)

$100 unauthorized tip + ~$83 unconfirmed airport pickup(s) + ~$83 Day Zero ride with no confirmed pickup = ~$266+ total conversion.

7. Tortious Interference with Contract

Threatened to leave clients stranded at 4AM, preventing timely use of remaining rides. Threat made to coerce compliance/payment.

8. Intentional Infliction of Emotional Distress

4AM abandonment threat at unfamiliar venue, exploitation of health vulnerability (recent cancer survivor celebration; travel-fatigued), pattern of gaslighting + intimidation.

9. CA Consumer Legal Remedies Act §1750

Deceptive practices (misrepresenting service terms, forfeiture policies, wait costs), unfair practices (eavesdropping, illegal recording, threats, gaslighting). Damages: Treble damages (3x) + attorney fees if you prevail.

10. Fraud in the Inducement

Repeated identical scheme (2025 + 2026) = premeditation. Induced contract based on false service level. Deliberately withheld forfeiture terms.

11. CCRA §51 Discrimination (Carlos)

Protected class status: Black male + cancer survivor. Eddie's documented knowledge + disparate treatment = violation. Damages: Treble damages (3x) + attorney fees + civil penalties.

12. Punitive Damages (CA Civil Code §3294)

Oppression: Deliberately targeted vulnerable person despite knowledge. Reckless disregard: Ignored illness; forced continued exertion. Fraud: Unilateral term changes; deliberate misrepresentation. Multiplier: 3-5x actual damages for oppressive conduct against vulnerable protected class member.

13. Lost Wages (Carlos)

Valley fever diagnosis + 1 week recovery. Missed work April 22-28, 2026. ~$1,000-1,200 (adjust to hourly rate × hours missed). Causation: Eddie's negligence caused illness → lost work.

14. Valley Fever Negligence (Medical Causation)

Coccidioidomycosis contracted via desert dust inhalation. Coachella = high endemic area. Excessive walking in dust = increased spore exposure. Post-cancer patients = compromised immunity. Eddie's refusal of inside-pickups = direct increased exposure. Causation chain: Degraded service → excessive walking → Valley fever → lost work.
